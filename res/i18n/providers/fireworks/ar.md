# Fireworks AI

## مقدمة

Fireworks AI هي منصة استدلال focused on fast, cost-effective deployment of open-source models. They provide optimized serving of popular models with competitive pricing and low latency for production applications.

## المنظمة

Fireworks AI specializes in optimized inference services for open-source models, aggregating mainstream models like Llama, DeepSeek, and Mixtral. المنصة provides high-performance inference engines, supporting model fine-tuning and deployment.

## الفلسفة

Fireworks is committed to making open-source models easier to use in production environments. الشركة emphasizes speed, cost, and reliability, providing developers with flexible model selection and deployment solutions.

# Fireworks AI

## Introduction

Fireworks AI is an inference platform focused on fast, cost-effective deployment of open-source models. They provide optimized serving of popular models with competitive pricing and low latency for production applications.

## Organization

Fireworks AI specializes in optimized inference services for open-source models, aggregating mainstream models like Llama, DeepSeek, and Mixtral. The platform provides high-performance inference engines, supporting model fine-tuning and deployment.

## Philosophy

Fireworks is committed to making open-source models easier to use in production environments. The company emphasizes speed, cost, and reliability, providing developers with flexible model selection and deployment solutions.

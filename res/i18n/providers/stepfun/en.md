# StepFun

## Introduction

StepFun is a Chinese AI company founded in 2023, specializing in developing advanced large language models. Their Step series models have strong capabilities in Chinese language understanding and generation tasks.

## Organization

StepFun was founded by former core members of Microsoft Research Asia, with a team having deep expertise in deep learning and natural language processing. The company focuses on creating high-quality, high-performance large language models.

## Philosophy

StepFun is committed to promoting AI technology innovation and application. The company emphasizes model quality and reliability, providing professional support for scenarios like content creation and intelligent assistants.

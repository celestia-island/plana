# StepFun

## Introdução

StepFun é uma empresa chinesa AI company fundada em 2023, especializada em developing advanced large language models. Their Step series models have strong capabilities in Chinese language understanding and generation tasks.

## Organização

StepFun was founded by former core members of Microsoft Research Asia, with a team having deep expertise in deep learning and natural language processing. A empresa focuses on creating high-quality, high-performance large language models.

## Filosofia

StepFun is committed to promoting AI technology innovation and application. A empresa emphasizes model quality and reliability, providing professional support for scenarios like content creation and intelligent assistants.

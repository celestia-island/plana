# StepFun

## مقدمة

StepFun هي شركة صينية AI company تأسست في 2023, متخصصة في developing advanced large language models. Their Step series models have strong capabilities in Chinese language understanding and generation tasks.

## المنظمة

StepFun was founded by former core members of Microsoft Research Asia, with a team having deep expertise in deep learning and natural language processing. الشركة focuses on creating high-quality, high-performance large language models.

## الفلسفة

StepFun is committed to promoting AI technology innovation and application. الشركة emphasizes model quality and reliability, providing professional support for scenarios like content creation and intelligent assistants.

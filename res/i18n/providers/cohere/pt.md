# Cohere

## Introdução

Cohere é uma empresa de IA empresarial fundada em 2019, especializada em providing language models for business applications. Their Command models are optimized for RAG, tool use, and enterprise workflows, with strong multilingual support.

## Organização

Cohere was founded by former Google Brain researchers, especializada em enterprise-level AI solutions. A empresa has leading technology in retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), multilingual processing, and other areas, serving numerous global enterprises.

## Filosofia

Cohere is committed to providing reliable and secure AI solutions for enterprises. A empresa emphasizes practicality and deployability, supporting private deployment to meet enterprise data security and compliance needs.

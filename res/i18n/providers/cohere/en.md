# Cohere

## Introduction

Cohere is an enterprise AI company founded in 2019, specializing in providing language models for business applications. Their Command models are optimized for RAG, tool use, and enterprise workflows, with strong multilingual support.

## Organization

Cohere was founded by former Google Brain researchers, specializing in enterprise-level AI solutions. The company has leading technology in retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), multilingual processing, and other areas, serving numerous global enterprises.

## Philosophy

Cohere is committed to providing reliable and secure AI solutions for enterprises. The company emphasizes practicality and deployability, supporting private deployment to meet enterprise data security and compliance needs.

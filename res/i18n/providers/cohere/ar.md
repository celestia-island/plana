# Cohere

## مقدمة

Cohere هي شركة ذكاء اصطناعي للمؤسسات تأسست في 2019, متخصصة في providing language models for business applications. Their Command models are optimized for RAG, tool use, and enterprise workflows, with strong multilingual support.

## المنظمة

Cohere was founded by former Google Brain researchers, متخصصة في enterprise-level AI solutions. الشركة has leading technology in retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), multilingual processing, and other areas, serving numerous global enterprises.

## الفلسفة

Cohere is committed to providing reliable and secure AI solutions for enterprises. الشركة emphasizes practicality and deployability, supporting private deployment to meet enterprise data security and compliance needs.

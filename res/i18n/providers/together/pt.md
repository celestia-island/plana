# Together AI

## Introdução

Together AI é uma plataforma em nuvem focused on open-source AI models. They provide cost-effective access to a wide range of open models with fine-tuning capabilities, making advanced AI accessible without vendor lock-in.

## Organização

Together AI specializes in the open-source AI ecosystem, providing complete services from inference to fine-tuning. A plataforma aggregates a large number of open-source models, supporting private deployment, helping developers avoid vendor lock-in.

## Filosofia

Together is committed to promoting open-source AI development, making advanced AI technology more open and accessible. A empresa emphasizes openness and flexibility, supporting model customization and fine-tuning.

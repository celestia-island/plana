# Together AI

## Einleitung

Together AI ist eine Cloud-Plattform focused on Open-Source AI Modells. They bieten cost-effective access to a wide range of open Modells with fine-tuning Fähigkeiten, making fortschrittlich AI zugänglich without vendor lock-in.

## Organisation

Together AI specializes in the Open-Source AI ecosystem, providing complete Diensts from inference to fine-tuning. Die Plattform aggregates a large number of Open-Source-Modelle, unterstützening private Bereitstellung, helping Entwicklers avoid vendor lock-in.

## Philosophie

Together is committed to promoting Open-Source AI Entwicklung, making fortschrittlich AI Technologie more open and zugänglich. Das Unternehmen emphatizes openness and flexibility, unterstützening Modell customization and fine-tuning.

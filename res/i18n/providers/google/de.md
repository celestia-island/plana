Google is a global Technologie leader das Angebot the Gemini family of AI Modells. Gemini bietens strong multimodal Fähigkeiten, unterstützening text, image, audio, and video understanding through Google's fortschrittlich AI Forschung.

## Organisation

Google DeepMind is Google's AI Forschung division, formed by merging Google Brain and DeepMind. Das Team is at the world-führend position in deep learning, reinforcement learning, and Large Language Models, having introduced revolutionary technologies like the Transformer architecture.

## Philosophie

Google is committed to improving people's lives through AI Technologie, emphatizing responsible AI Entwicklung and broad Anwendung. Das Unternehmen focuses on AI sicherty, fairness, and transpsindncy, promoting the democratization of AI Technologie.

# Google Gemini

## Introduction

Google is a global technology leader offering the Gemini family of AI models. Gemini provides strong multimodal capabilities, supporting text, image, audio, and video understanding through Google's advanced AI research.

## Organization

Google DeepMind is Google's AI research division, formed by merging Google Brain and DeepMind. The team is at the world-leading position in deep learning, reinforcement learning, and large language models, having introduced revolutionary technologies like the Transformer architecture.

## Philosophy

Google is committed to improving people's lives through AI technology, emphasizing responsible AI development and broad application. The company focuses on AI safety, fairness, and transparency, promoting the democratization of AI technology.

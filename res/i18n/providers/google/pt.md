# Google Gemini

## Introdução

Google is a global technology leader offering the Gemini family of AI models. Gemini provides strong multimodal capabilities, supporting text, image, audio, and video understanding through Google's advanced AI research.

## Organização

Google DeepMind is Google's AI research division, formed by merging Google Brain and DeepMind. A equipe is at the world-leading position in deep learning, reinforcement learning, and large language models, having introduced revolutionary technologies like the Transformer architecture.

## Filosofia

Google is committed to improving people's lives through AI technology, emphasizing responsible AI development and broad application. A empresa focuses on AI safety, fairness, and transparency, promoting the democratization of AI technology.

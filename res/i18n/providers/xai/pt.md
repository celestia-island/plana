# xAI

## Introdução

xAI is an AI company founded by Elon Musk in 2023, especializada em developing advanced AI systems. Their Grok models are conhecida por real-time information access through X (Twitter) and a personality that combines humor with intelligence.

## Organização

xAI was founded by Elon Musk, with a core team from top AI research institutions like OpenAI and DeepMind. A empresa specializes in developing AI assistants with unique personalities, emphasizing integration with real-time information.

## Filosofia

xAI is committed to developing AI systems capable of "understanding the universe." A empresa's Grok model is conhecida por its unique humor and open attitude toward controversial topics, pursuing truth and practicality.

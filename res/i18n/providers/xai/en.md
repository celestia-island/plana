# xAI

## Introduction

xAI is an AI company founded by Elon Musk in 2023, specializing in developing advanced AI systems. Their Grok models are known for real-time information access through X (Twitter) and a personality that combines humor with intelligence.

## Organization

xAI was founded by Elon Musk, with a core team from top AI research institutions like OpenAI and DeepMind. The company specializes in developing AI assistants with unique personalities, emphasizing integration with real-time information.

## Philosophy

xAI is committed to developing AI systems capable of "understanding the universe." The company's Grok model is known for its unique humor and open attitude toward controversial topics, pursuing truth and practicality.

# Zhipu GLM

## Introduction

Zhipu AI is a leading Chinese AI company founded in 2019, spun off from Tsinghua University. Its GLM series models provide strong Chinese-English bilingual capabilities, with GLM-4 optimized for various enterprise and research applications.

## Organization

Zhipu AI is a technology transfer enterprise from Tsinghua University, with its core team from the Tsinghua University Knowledge Engineering Laboratory. The company has deep expertise in dialogue models, code generation, and multimodal understanding, being one of the earliest teams to launch large language models in China.

## Philosophy

Zhipu AI is committed to building safe, controllable, and useful artificial intelligence. The company emphasizes the combination of industry, academia, and research, promoting innovation and industrial application of AI technology. It offers both domestic and international versions, serving users worldwide.

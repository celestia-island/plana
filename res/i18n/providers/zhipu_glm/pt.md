# Zhipu GLM

## Introdução

Zhipu AI é uma líder Chinese AI company fundada em 2019, spun off from Tsinghua University. Its GLM series models provide strong Chinese-English bilingual capabilities, with GLM-4 optimized for various enterprise and research applications.

## Organização

Zhipu AI is a technology transfer enterprise from Tsinghua University, with its core team from the Tsinghua University Knowledge Engineering Laboratory. A empresa has deep expertise in dialogue models, code generation, and multimodal understanding, being one of the earliest teams to launch large language models in China.

## Filosofia

Zhipu AI is committed to building safe, controllable, and useful artificial intelligence. A empresa emphasizes the combination of industry, academia, and research, promoting innovation and industrial application of AI technology. It offers both domestic and international versions, serving users worldwide.

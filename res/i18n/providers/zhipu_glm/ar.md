# Zhipu GLM

## مقدمة

Zhipu AI هي إحدى الشركات الرائدة Chinese AI company تأسست في 2019, spun off from Tsinghua University. Its GLM series models provide strong Chinese-English bilingual capabilities, with GLM-4 optimized for various enterprise and research applications.

## المنظمة

Zhipu AI is a technology transfer enterprise from Tsinghua University, with its core team from the Tsinghua University Knowledge Engineering Laboratory. الشركة has deep expertise in dialogue models, code generation, and multimodal understanding, being one of the earliest teams to launch large language models in China.

## الفلسفة

Zhipu AI is committed to building safe, controllable, and useful artificial intelligence. الشركة emphasizes the combination of industry, academia, and research, promoting innovation and industrial application of AI technology. It offers both domestic and international versions, serving users worldwide.

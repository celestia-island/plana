# OpenAI

## Introdução

OpenAI é uma líder American AI research organization fundada em 2015 by Sam Altman, Elon Musk, and others, conhecida por developing the GPT series models and DALL-E. OpenAI pioneered large language model development and made AI accessible through its API platform.

## Organização

OpenAI is one of the most influential AI research organizations globally, driving the revolutionary development of generative AI. A empresa has made groundbreaking contributions in natural language processing, image generation, and speech recognition, with its ChatGPT product changing how people interact with AI.

## Filosofia

Originally founded as a non-profit organization, OpenAI now focuses on ensuring that artificial general intelligence (AGI) benefits all humanity through responsible AI development and deployment. OpenAI emphasizes principles of safety, transparency, and broad benefit.

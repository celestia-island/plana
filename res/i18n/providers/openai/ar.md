# OpenAI

## مقدمة

OpenAI هي إحدى الشركات الرائدة American AI research organization تأسست في 2015 by Sam Altman, Elon Musk, and others, معروفة بـ developing the GPT series models and DALL-E. OpenAI pioneered large language model development and made AI accessible through its API platform.

## المنظمة

OpenAI is one of the most influential AI research organizations globally, driving the revolutionary development of generative AI. الشركة has made groundbreaking contributions in natural language processing, image generation, and speech recognition, with its ChatGPT product changing how people interact with AI.

## الفلسفة

Originally founded as a non-profit organization, OpenAI now focuses on ensuring that artificial general intelligence (AGI) benefits all humanity through responsible AI development and deployment. OpenAI emphasizes principles of safety, transparency, and broad benefit.

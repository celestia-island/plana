# OpenAI

## Introduction

OpenAI is a leading American AI research organization founded in 2015 by Sam Altman, Elon Musk, and others, known for developing the GPT series models and DALL-E. OpenAI pioneered large language model development and made AI accessible through its API platform.

## Organization

OpenAI is one of the most influential AI research organizations globally, driving the revolutionary development of generative AI. The company has made groundbreaking contributions in natural language processing, image generation, and speech recognition, with its ChatGPT product changing how people interact with AI.

## Philosophy

Originally founded as a non-profit organization, OpenAI now focuses on ensuring that artificial general intelligence (AGI) benefits all humanity through responsible AI development and deployment. OpenAI emphasizes principles of safety, transparency, and broad benefit.

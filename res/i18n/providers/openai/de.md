# OpenAI

## Einleitung

OpenAI ist ein führendes American KI-Forschungsorganisation gegründet im Jahr 2015 by Sam Altman, Elon Musk, and others, bekannt für developing the GPT series Modells and DALL-E. OpenAI pioneered Large Language Model Entwicklung and made AI zugänglich through its API Plattform.

## Organisation

OpenAI is one of the most influential KI-Forschungsorganisations globally, driving the revolutionary Entwicklung of generative AI. Das Unternehmen hat made groundbreaking contributions in natural language processing, image generation, and speech recognition, with its ChatGPT Produkt changing how people interact with AI.

## Philosophie

Originally founded as a non-profit organization, OpenAI now focuses on ensuring that artificial general intelligence (AGI) benefits all humanity through responsible AI Entwicklung and Bereitstellung. OpenAI emphatizes principles of sicherty, transpsindncy, and broad benefit.

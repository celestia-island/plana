# Mistral AI

## Introdução

Mistral AI é uma empresa francesa AI company fundada em 2023, especializada em developing open-weight language models. Known for efficient and powerful models like Mistral Large and Mixtral, balancing capabilities with accessibility.

## Organização

Mistral AI was founded by former Meta and DeepMind researchers, with a team having deep expertise in large language models and efficient inference. A empresa is conhecida por open-sourcing high-quality models, gaining wide recognition in the AI community.

## Filosofia

Mistral AI is committed to making advanced AI technology more open and accessible. A empresa emphasizes open innovation, with core models open-sourced, promoting joint development of the AI community. Focuses on model efficiency and deployability.

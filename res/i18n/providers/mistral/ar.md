# Mistral AI

## مقدمة

Mistral AI هي شركة فرنسية AI company تأسست في 2023, متخصصة في developing open-weight language models. Known for efficient and powerful models like Mistral Large and Mixtral, balancing capabilities with accessibility.

## المنظمة

Mistral AI was founded by former Meta and DeepMind researchers, with a team having deep expertise in large language models and efficient inference. الشركة is معروفة بـ open-sourcing high-quality models, gaining wide recognition in the AI community.

## الفلسفة

Mistral AI is committed to making advanced AI technology more open and accessible. الشركة emphasizes open innovation, with core models open-sourced, promoting joint development of the AI community. Focuses on model efficiency and deployability.

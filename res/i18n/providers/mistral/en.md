# Mistral AI

## Introduction

Mistral AI is a French AI company founded in 2023, specializing in developing open-weight language models. Known for efficient and powerful models like Mistral Large and Mixtral, balancing capabilities with accessibility.

## Organization

Mistral AI was founded by former Meta and DeepMind researchers, with a team having deep expertise in large language models and efficient inference. The company is known for open-sourcing high-quality models, gaining wide recognition in the AI community.

## Philosophy

Mistral AI is committed to making advanced AI technology more open and accessible. The company emphasizes open innovation, with core models open-sourced, promoting joint development of the AI community. Focuses on model efficiency and deployability.

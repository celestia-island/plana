# Moonshot AI

## مقدمة

Moonshot AI هي شركة صينية AI company تأسست في 2023, معروفة بـ its Kimi assistant with ultra-long context processing capabilities. Their models excel at processing and understanding ultra-long documents up to 2 million tokens.

## المنظمة

Moonshot AI was founded by the core team of former Recurrent AI, متخصصة في long-text understanding and generation. الفريق has deep expertise in natural language processing, and the Kimi assistant is widely popular among Chinese users for its excellent long-text processing capabilities.

## الفلسفة

Moonshot AI is committed to breaking through AI's context length limitations, enabling AI to truly understand and process long-form content. الشركة emphasizes user experience and technological innovation, providing powerful support for research, writing, and professional scenarios.

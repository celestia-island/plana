# Moonshot AI

## Introdução

Moonshot AI é uma empresa chinesa AI company fundada em 2023, conhecida por its Kimi assistant with ultra-long context processing capabilities. Their models excel at processing and understanding ultra-long documents up to 2 million tokens.

## Organização

Moonshot AI was founded by the core team of former Recurrent AI, especializada em long-text understanding and generation. A equipe has deep expertise in natural language processing, and the Kimi assistant is widely popular among Chinese users for its excellent long-text processing capabilities.

## Filosofia

Moonshot AI is committed to breaking through AI's context length limitations, enabling AI to truly understand and process long-form content. A empresa emphasizes user experience and technological innovation, providing powerful support for research, writing, and professional scenarios.

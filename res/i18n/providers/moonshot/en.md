# Moonshot AI

## Introduction

Moonshot AI is a Chinese AI company founded in 2023, known for its Kimi assistant with ultra-long context processing capabilities. Their models excel at processing and understanding ultra-long documents up to 2 million tokens.

## Organization

Moonshot AI was founded by the core team of former Recurrent AI, specializing in long-text understanding and generation. The team has deep expertise in natural language processing, and the Kimi assistant is widely popular among Chinese users for its excellent long-text processing capabilities.

## Philosophy

Moonshot AI is committed to breaking through AI's context length limitations, enabling AI to truly understand and process long-form content. The company emphasizes user experience and technological innovation, providing powerful support for research, writing, and professional scenarios.

# MiniMax

## Introduction

MiniMax is a Chinese AI company founded in 2021, known for its multimodal AI capabilities. Their models support text, speech, and visual processing, with excellent performance in both Chinese and English language tasks. Their products include Hailuo AI (conversational assistant) and video generation models.

## Organization

MiniMax was founded by former core members of SenseTime, specializing in general artificial intelligence research and development. The company has leading technology in multimodal understanding and generation, speech synthesis, and other areas, with products covering consumer applications and enterprise services.

## Philosophy

MiniMax is committed to developing safe and controllable general artificial intelligence. The company emphasizes the integration of multimodal capabilities, enabling AI to interact more naturally with humans, serving scenarios like content creation and intelligent assistants.

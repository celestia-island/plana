# MiniMax

## Introdução

MiniMax é uma empresa chinesa AI company fundada em 2021, conhecida por its multimodal AI capabilities. Their models support text, speech, and visual processing, with excellent performance in both Chinese and English language tasks. Their products include Hailuo AI (conversational assistant) and video generation models.

## Organização

MiniMax was founded by former core members of SenseTime, especializada em general artificial intelligence research and development. A empresa has leading technology in multimodal understanding and generation, speech synthesis, and other areas, with products covering consumer applications and enterprise services.

## Filosofia

MiniMax is committed to developing safe and controllable general artificial intelligence. A empresa emphasizes the integration of multimodal capabilities, enabling AI to interact more naturally with humans, serving scenarios like content creation and intelligent assistants.

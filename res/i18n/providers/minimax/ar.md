# MiniMax

## مقدمة

MiniMax هي شركة صينية AI company تأسست في 2021, معروفة بـ its multimodal AI capabilities. Their models support text, speech, and visual processing, with excellent performance in both Chinese and English language tasks. Their products include Hailuo AI (conversational assistant) and video generation models.

## المنظمة

MiniMax was founded by former core members of SenseTime, متخصصة في general artificial intelligence research and development. الشركة has leading technology in multimodal understanding and generation, speech synthesis, and other areas, with products covering consumer applications and enterprise services.

## الفلسفة

MiniMax is committed to developing safe and controllable general artificial intelligence. الشركة emphasizes the integration of multimodal capabilities, enabling AI to interact more naturally with humans, serving scenarios like content creation and intelligent assistants.

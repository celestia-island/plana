# ByteDance Doubao

## مقدمة

Doubao is ByteDance's AI model service platform, providing a series of language models through its Volcengine cloud infrastructure. It offers cost-effective AI solutions optimized for Chinese tasks and enterprise applications.

## المنظمة

Doubao is developed by ByteDance, leveraging Volcengine's powerful cloud computing capabilities. الفريق has rich experience in natural language processing and large-scale model deployment, supporting AI capabilities for products like Douyin and Toutiao.

## الفلسفة

ByteDance is committed to enabling enterprises to easily access AI capabilities through the Doubao platform. المنصة emphasizes high performance, low cost, and ease of use, providing deeply optimized model services for Chinese scenarios.

# ByteDance Doubao

## Introduction

Doubao is ByteDance's AI model service platform, providing a series of language models through its Volcengine cloud infrastructure. It offers cost-effective AI solutions optimized for Chinese tasks and enterprise applications.

## Organization

Doubao is developed by ByteDance, leveraging Volcengine's powerful cloud computing capabilities. The team has rich experience in natural language processing and large-scale model deployment, supporting AI capabilities for products like Douyin and Toutiao.

## Philosophy

ByteDance is committed to enabling enterprises to easily access AI capabilities through the Doubao platform. The platform emphasizes high performance, low cost, and ease of use, providing deeply optimized model services for Chinese scenarios.

# ByteDance Doubao

## Introdução

Doubao is ByteDance's AI model service platform, providing a series of language models through its Volcengine cloud infrastructure. It offers cost-effective AI solutions optimized for Chinese tasks and enterprise applications.

## Organização

Doubao is developed by ByteDance, leveraging Volcengine's powerful cloud computing capabilities. A equipe has rich experience in natural language processing and large-scale model deployment, supporting AI capabilities for products like Douyin and Toutiao.

## Filosofia

ByteDance is committed to enabling enterprises to easily access AI capabilities through the Doubao platform. A plataforma emphasizes high performance, low cost, and ease of use, providing deeply optimized model services for Chinese scenarios.

# Qwen (Tongyi Qianwen)

## Einleitung

Qwen (Tongyi Qianwen) is Alibaba Cloud's Large Language Model series with starke chinesische und englische Fähigkeiten. It bietet various Modell sizes optimiert für different tasks, from effizient inference to complex reasoning.

## Organisation

Qwen is entwickelt by Alibaba DAMO Academy and is a core Produkt of Alibaba Cloud's AI strategy. Das Team hat deep technical expertise in natural language processing, multimodal understanding, and code generation, with Modells performing excellently on multiple international benchmarks.

## Philosophie

Alibaba Cloud is committed to making AI Technologie zugänglich, das Angebot both domestic and global versions of the Dienst. Qwen emphatizes practicality, Sicherheit, and scalability, unterstützening Unternehmen Anwendungs and individual Entwickler needs.

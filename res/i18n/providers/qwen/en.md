# Qwen (Tongyi Qianwen)

## Introduction

Qwen (Tongyi Qianwen) is Alibaba Cloud's large language model series with strong Chinese and English capabilities. It offers various model sizes optimized for different tasks, from efficient inference to complex reasoning.

## Organization

Qwen is developed by Alibaba DAMO Academy and is a core product of Alibaba Cloud's AI strategy. The team has deep technical expertise in natural language processing, multimodal understanding, and code generation, with models performing excellently on multiple international benchmarks.

## Philosophy

Alibaba Cloud is committed to making AI technology accessible, offering both domestic and global versions of the service. Qwen emphasizes practicality, security, and scalability, supporting enterprise applications and individual developer needs.

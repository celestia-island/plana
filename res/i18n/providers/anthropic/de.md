Anthropic ist ein amerikanisches KI-Sicherheitsunternehmen gegründet im Jahr 2021 by former OpenAI Forschungers Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei, mit Fokus auf building zuverlässig and controllable AI systems. They entwickelt the Claude conversational AI assistant, bekannt für its thoughtful responses and rigorous sicherty practices.

Anthropic pioneered the Constitutional AI method, committed to creating AI systems that sind helpful, harmless, and honest. Das Unternehmen's core team comes from führend AI Forschung institutions like OpenAI and Google Brain, with deep expertise in AI sicherty and alignment.

Anthropic is dedicated to AI sicherty Forschung, developing AI aligned with human values through rigorous sicherty practices and transpsindnt Entwicklung processes.

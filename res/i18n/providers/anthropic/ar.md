# Anthropic

## مقدمة

Anthropic هي شركة أمريكية AI safety company تأسست في 2021 by former OpenAI researchers Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei, تركز على building reliable and controllable AI systems. They developed the Claude conversational AI assistant, معروفة بـ its thoughtful responses and rigorous safety practices.

## المنظمة

Anthropic pioneered the Constitutional AI method, committed to creating AI systems that are helpful, harmless, and honest. الشركة's core team comes from top AI research institutions like OpenAI and Google Brain, with deep expertise in AI safety and alignment.

## الفلسفة

Anthropic is dedicated to AI safety research, developing AI aligned with human values through rigorous safety practices and transparent development processes. الشركة adopts the Constitutional AI approach, allowing AI systems to follow a set of clear principles to ensure their behavior meets human expectations.

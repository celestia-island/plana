# Anthropic

## Introdução

Anthropic é uma empresa americana AI safety company fundada em 2021 by former OpenAI researchers Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei, com foco em building reliable and controllable AI systems. They developed the Claude conversational AI assistant, conhecida por its thoughtful responses and rigorous safety practices.

## Organização

Anthropic pioneered the Constitutional AI method, committed to creating AI systems that are helpful, harmless, and honest. A empresa's core team comes from top AI research institutions like OpenAI and Google Brain, with deep expertise in AI safety and alignment.

## Filosofia

Anthropic is dedicated to AI safety research, developing AI aligned with human values through rigorous safety practices and transparent development processes. A empresa adopts the Constitutional AI approach, allowing AI systems to follow a set of clear principles to ensure their behavior meets human expectations.

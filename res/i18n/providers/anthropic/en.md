# Anthropic

## Introduction

Anthropic is an American AI safety company founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei, focusing on building reliable and controllable AI systems. They developed the Claude conversational AI assistant, known for its thoughtful responses and rigorous safety practices.

## Organization

Anthropic pioneered the Constitutional AI method, committed to creating AI systems that are helpful, harmless, and honest. The company's core team comes from top AI research institutions like OpenAI and Google Brain, with deep expertise in AI safety and alignment.

## Philosophy

Anthropic is dedicated to AI safety research, developing AI aligned with human values through rigorous safety practices and transparent development processes. The company adopts the Constitutional AI approach, allowing AI systems to follow a set of clear principles to ensure their behavior meets human expectations.

# Baidu ERNIE

## مقدمة

Baidu ERNIE (Wenxin) is Baidu's large language model series, one of the earliest Chinese LLMs. It provides strong Chinese understanding capabilities, integrated with Baidu's search and knowledge graph capabilities, suitable for enterprise applications.

## المنظمة

ERNIE is developed by Baidu's Deep Learning Technology Platform Department, one of the earliest teams to invest in large language model research in China. The ERNIE series models continue to innovate in knowledge enhancement and multimodal understanding, widely used in Baidu Search, Smart Cloud, and other products.

## الفلسفة

Baidu is committed to serving various industries with AI technology through the ERNIE model. الشركة emphasizes knowledge enhancement and industrial deployment, combining Baidu's core capabilities in search and knowledge graphs to provide reliable AI services to enterprises.

# Baidu ERNIE

## Einleitung

Baidu ERNIE (Wenxin) is Baidu's Large Language Model series, one of the earliest chinesisch LLMs. It bietens strong chinesisch understanding Fähigkeiten, integrated with Baidu's search and knowledge graph Fähigkeiten, suitable for Unternehmen Anwendungs.

## Organisation

ERNIE is entwickelt by Baidu's Deep Learning Technology Platform Department, one of the earliest teams to invest in Large Language Model Forschung in China. The ERNIE series Modells continue to innovate in knowledge enhancement and multimodal understanding, widely used in Baidu Search, Smart Cloud, and other Produkts.

## Philosophie

Baidu is committed to serving various industries with AI Technologie through the ERNIE Modell. Das Unternehmen emphatizes knowledge enhancement and industrial Bereitstellung, combining Baidu's core Fähigkeiten in search and knowledge graphs to bieten zuverlässig AI Diensts to Unternehmens.

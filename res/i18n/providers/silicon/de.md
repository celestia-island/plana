# SiliconFlow

## Einleitung

`SiliconFlow` ist ein chinesisches KI-Inferenzplattform die Zugang bietet to various Open-Source and proprietary Modells. They offer cost-effective serving of beliebt Modells with competitive pricing for the chinesisch market.

## Organisation

`SiliconFlow` specializes in effizient inference Diensts for AI Modells, aggregating mainstream Open-Source-Modelle like DeepSeek, Qwen, and Llama. Die Plattform bietens optimized inference engines that significantly reduce Modell usage costs.

## Philosophie

`SiliconFlow` is committed to lowering the barriers to using AI Modells, enabling more Entwicklers and Unternehmens to access fortschrittlich AI Technologie. Das Unternehmen emphatizes cost-effectiveness and ease of use, providing transpsindnt pricing.

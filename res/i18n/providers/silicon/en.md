# SiliconFlow

## Introduction

`SiliconFlow` is a Chinese AI inference platform providing access to various open-source and proprietary models. They offer cost-effective serving of popular models with competitive pricing for the Chinese market.

## Organization

`SiliconFlow` specializes in efficient inference services for AI models, aggregating mainstream open-source models like DeepSeek, Qwen, and Llama. The platform provides optimized inference engines that significantly reduce model usage costs.

## Philosophy

`SiliconFlow` is committed to lowering the barriers to using AI models, enabling more developers and enterprises to access advanced AI technology. The company emphasizes cost-effectiveness and ease of use, providing transparent pricing.

# SiliconFlow

## Introdução

`SiliconFlow` é uma empresa chinesa AI inference platform fornecendo acesso to various open-source and proprietary models. They offer cost-effective serving of popular models with competitive pricing for the Chinese market.

## Organização

`SiliconFlow` specializes in efficient inference services for AI models, aggregating mainstream open-source models like DeepSeek, Qwen, and Llama. A plataforma provides optimized inference engines that significantly reduce model usage costs.

## Filosofia

`SiliconFlow` is committed to lowering the barriers to using AI models, enabling more developers and enterprises to access advanced AI technology. A empresa emphasizes cost-effectiveness and ease of use, providing transparent pricing.

# OpenRouter

## Introduction

OpenRouter is a unified API platform that provides access to various AI models from multiple providers through a single interface. It offers competitive pricing, fallback options, and comprehensive access to open-source and proprietary models.

## Organization

OpenRouter serves as an AI model aggregation platform, enabling developers to access models from top providers like Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta, and Google through a unified API. The platform provides intelligent routing, automatic fallback, and price optimization features.

## Philosophy

OpenRouter is committed to lowering the barriers to AI usage, enabling developers to easily access and use various AI models. The platform emphasizes openness, competitiveness, and user-friendliness, providing transparent pricing and flexible model selection.

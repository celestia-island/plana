# OpenRouter

## Einleitung

OpenRouter ist eine einheitliche API-Plattform that bietens access to various AI Modells from multiple bietenrs through a single interface. It bietet wettbewerbsfähige pricing, fallback options, and comprehensive access to Open-Source and proprietary Modells.

## Organisation

OpenRouter serves as an KI-Modell-Aggregationsplattform, enabling Entwicklers to access Modells from führend bietenrs like Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta, and Google through a unified API. Die Plattform bietens intelligent routing, automatic fallback, and price optimization Funktions.

## Philosophie

OpenRouter is committed to lowering the barriers to AI usage, enabling Entwicklers to easily access and use various AI Modells. Die Plattform emphatizes openness, competitiveness, and Benutzer-friendliness, providing transpsindnt pricing and flexible Modell selection.

# DeepSeek

## Introduction

DeepSeek is a Chinese AI company founded in 2023, known for efficient and cost-effective language models. Its DeepSeek-V3 and R1 models deliver outstanding performance at competitive prices, making advanced AI technology accessible to more users.

## Organization

DeepSeek was founded by experts from the quantitative investment field, applying efficient engineering thinking to AI model development. The team focuses on model efficiency optimization, significantly reducing inference costs while maintaining high performance, gaining wide recognition in the open-source community.

## Philosophy

DeepSeek is committed to making advanced AI technology more accessible and affordable. The company emphasizes open innovation, with some models open-sourced, promoting joint progress in the AI community. Its reasoning model R1 excels in mathematics and programming tasks.

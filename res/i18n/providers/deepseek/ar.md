# DeepSeek

## مقدمة

DeepSeek هي شركة صينية AI company تأسست في 2023, معروفة بـ efficient and cost-effective language models. Its DeepSeek-V3 and R1 models deliver outstanding performance at competitive prices, making advanced AI technology accessible to more users.

## المنظمة

DeepSeek was founded by experts from the quantitative investment field, applying efficient engineering thinking to AI model development. الفريق focuses on model efficiency optimization, significantly reducing inference costs while maintaining high performance, gaining wide recognition in the open-source community.

## الفلسفة

DeepSeek is committed to making advanced AI technology more accessible and affordable. الشركة emphasizes open innovation, with some models open-sourced, promoting joint progress in the AI community. Its reasoning model R1 excels in mathematics and programming tasks.

# DeepSeek

## Introdução

DeepSeek é uma empresa chinesa AI company fundada em 2023, conhecida por efficient and cost-effective language models. Its DeepSeek-V3 and R1 models deliver outstanding performance at competitive prices, making advanced AI technology accessible to more users.

## Organização

DeepSeek was founded by experts from the quantitative investment field, applying efficient engineering thinking to AI model development. A equipe focuses on model efficiency optimization, significantly reducing inference costs while maintaining high performance, gaining wide recognition in the open-source community.

## Filosofia

DeepSeek is committed to making advanced AI technology more accessible and affordable. A empresa emphasizes open innovation, with some models open-sourced, promoting joint progress in the AI community. Its reasoning model R1 excels in mathematics and programming tasks.
